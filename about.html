Our Background & Mission

🏛️

Company Background

Founded in 2026 inside the BERC2393 Internet And Multimedia class, TechRepairHub was built to combat electronic waste and preserve the right to repair to our customers. We recognized a critical need for transparent, original hardware servicing in a market that is filled with shady unauthorized repair setups.

🎯

Our Mission

To reduce global e-waste by maximizing device lifespans because repairing them is cheaper instead of buying new device everytime its broken. We achieve this through repasting thermals, motherboard fixing, component upgrades and stopping device from bootlooping which saves our clients money while protecting the environment.
